Step 5 · Sales Rep Portal

Goals, Accounts And Commission For Every Rep.

A login for territory reps, 1099 reps and rep agencies. Each rep sets a year goal when they join, tracks it on their dashboard, works only their assigned accounts, logs visits, sees orders and commission, plans with the income calculator and climbs the leaderboard. LTC sets monthly goals, rates and account assignments under Sales Team in the admin. Use “View As” to see a brand-new rep setting their goal.
