1. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, company, email, phone number, shipping and billing addresses, order details, messages you send us, and documents you upload (for example, a resale or tax-exempt certificate). Payment card details are entered on and processed by Shopify and its payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.

We also collect basic technical information automatically, such as browser type, device, pages viewed and referring site, through cookies and similar tools. Our site saves your cart in your browser so it is still there when you come back.

2. How We Use Information

  • To take, ship and support your orders, including returns and warranty questions.
  • To answer messages, quotes and calls.
  • To run partner, affiliate and rep accounts, including pricing, invoices, commissions and tax-exempt status.
  • To send order updates and, if you opt in, news and offers. You can opt out at any time.
  • To keep the Services secure, prevent fraud, and improve our site and products.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as Shopify (store and checkout), payment processors, shipping carriers, email and text providers, and our accountants, and only for those purposes. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or others’ safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business.

4. Cookies

We use cookies and browser storage to keep your cart, remember choices and understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the site, such as the cart, may not work without them.

5. Your Choices And Rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop marketing messages, by contacting us below. Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, including the right to know what we collect and the right not to be treated differently for using your rights. We will verify requests before acting on them.

6. Text Messages

If you text us or agree to receive texts, message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We do not share mobile numbers with third parties for their marketing.

7. Data Security And Retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure. We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above, including tax and accounting record-keeping, and then delete or anonymize it.

8. Children

The Services are for businesses and adult buyers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the Services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
