Affiliate & Brand Ambassador Agreement · Version 1 (Nov 2026)

This Affiliate & Brand Ambassador Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Liberty Industrial Coating Systems Inc., doing business as Liberty Tool Company ("LTC," "Company," "we," or "us"), and the undersigned individual or business ("Affiliate," "Ambassador," or "you"). By signing this Agreement, Affiliate agrees to the following terms governing participation in the Liberty Tool Company Affiliate Program ("Program").

  • You earn $10 for every eligible Tape Caddy your link or code sells. A kit counts each caddy inside it.

1. Program Overview

The Program allows approved Affiliates to promote Liberty Tool Company products, including The Tape Caddy, through personal referrals, professional networks, digital marketing, social media, and other approved promotional channels.

Affiliates may earn commissions on qualifying product sales attributed to their assigned affiliate links, promotional codes, or other Company-approved tracking methods.

Participation does not grant Affiliate exclusive sales rights, a distribution territory, or authority to represent LTC in contractual negotiations.

LTC reserves the right to approve, decline, suspend, or terminate participation in the Program in accordance with this Agreement.

2. Affiliate Compensation

2.1 Commission Rate

Affiliate shall receive $10.00 USD for each eligible individual Tape Caddy unit sold through an approved and properly attributed referral.

Commissions are calculated based on the number of eligible Tape Caddy units purchased, not the number of orders placed.

For illustration:

  • One Tape Caddy sold: $10 commission.
  • Three individual Tape Caddies sold: $30 commission.
  • One Triple Threat bundle containing three eligible Tape Caddies: $30 commission.

Other products, accessories, and future product categories are not commissionable unless LTC expressly establishes their eligibility and commission rates in writing.

2.2 Qualifying Sales

A sale qualifies for commission when:

  • 1. The customer completes a purchase through an LTC-approved sales channel.
  • 2. The transaction is successfully attributed to Affiliate through an approved tracking method.
  • 3. LTC receives payment for the order.
  • 4. The transaction is not canceled, refunded, fraudulent, or otherwise disqualified under this Agreement.

Sales generated through unauthorized marketplaces, manipulated tracking systems, or prohibited promotional activities are not eligible for commission.

2.3 Monthly Payments

Affiliate commissions shall be calculated and paid once per calendar month, following LTC's reconciliation of eligible sales.

LTC may withhold commissions associated with pending refunds, chargebacks, disputed transactions, suspected fraud, or incomplete payment information until the matter is resolved.

Any commissions previously paid on refunded or reversed transactions may be deducted from future payouts or recovered where legally permitted.

Affiliate is responsible for providing accurate payment information and any required tax documentation.

2.4 Commission Adjustments

LTC may modify commission rates for future transactions upon written or electronic notice to Affiliate. Changes shall not retroactively reduce commissions already earned on qualifying transactions.

3. Affiliate Links & Sales Tracking

LTC will provide Affiliate with one or more approved tracking methods, which may include unique referral links, promotional codes, or access to an affiliate portal.

Affiliate agrees to use only Company-authorized tracking methods when promoting LTC products.

LTC's designated transaction and attribution records shall serve as the primary basis for commission calculations, subject to correction of demonstrated errors.

Affiliate shall not manipulate referral links, create fraudulent transactions, artificially inflate sales, or engage in unauthorized self-referrals.

LTC may review and correct tracking discrepancies. Affiliate may submit a written commission dispute within thirty (30) days after receiving the applicable payout statement.

LTC does not guarantee that every introduction or customer interaction will result in a commissionable sale.

4. Marketing & Brand Guidelines

Affiliate agrees to represent Liberty Tool Company and its products accurately, professionally, and consistently with the Company's brand standards.

Affiliate shall:

  • Use approved LTC logos, product photographs, marketing materials, and brand assets.
  • Follow LTC's current Brand Guidelines and written promotional instructions.
  • Accurately describe product functionality, benefits, specifications, pricing, and availability.
  • Clearly identify their affiliate relationship when promoting LTC products.
  • Obtain written approval before using LTC trademarks in paid advertisements, website domains, or other promotional materials requiring Company authorization.
  • Comply with applicable advertising, consumer protection, privacy, and intellectual property laws.

Affiliate shall not:

  • Alter LTC trademarks or logos without authorization.
  • Make misleading product claims, unverified performance guarantees, or unsupported earnings claims.
  • Suggest that Affiliate is an employee, authorized distributor, or official spokesperson of LTC.
  • Use deceptive advertising, unsolicited bulk messaging, or misleading promotional tactics.
  • Represent or imply an endorsement by Sherwin-Williams or any other LTC business partner without express written authorization.

Affiliate shall clearly and conspicuously disclose material connections to LTC in endorsements and promotional content, including commission arrangements, as required by the Federal Trade Commission and other applicable laws.

LTC may require Affiliate to correct or remove promotional content that violates this Agreement or the Company's brand standards.

5. Amazon & Third-Party Marketplace Restrictions

Affiliate is strictly prohibited from independently listing, selling, reselling, or distributing LTC products through Amazon or other third-party marketplaces without prior written authorization from LTC.

Affiliate shall not:

  • Create unauthorized Amazon product listings or storefronts for LTC products.
  • Represent themselves as an authorized Amazon seller or distributor of LTC products.
  • Redirect customers to unauthorized third-party product listings.
  • Use LTC trademarks or product images in unauthorized marketplace listings.
  • Purchase LTC products for unauthorized marketplace resale.

Affiliate may promote an LTC-approved marketplace listing only when expressly authorized by LTC and when the associated referral method complies with the marketplace's applicable rules.

Violation of this section may result in immediate suspension or termination and forfeiture of commissions arising from the prohibited activity, to the extent permitted by law.

6. Customer Relationships & Referral Conduct

Affiliate is encouraged to introduce LTC products to installers, contractors, flooring professionals, supply houses, businesses, and other relevant audiences.

Affiliate agrees to conduct all outreach professionally and in compliance with applicable laws.

All purchases, fulfillment obligations, customer service matters, warranties, and returns shall be handled by LTC or its authorized sales partners.

Affiliate may not negotiate binding product prices, offer unauthorized discounts, provide warranties, accept payments on behalf of LTC, or enter into agreements binding the Company.

Affiliate has no ownership interest in LTC customers or customer accounts by virtue of participation in the Program.

7. Affiliate Team Referrals

LTC may permit Affiliates to introduce additional individuals or businesses to the Program.

Any such introductions shall be subject to LTC's approval and internal tracking procedures.

This Agreement does not establish a multilevel marketing program, downline commission structure, or entitlement to compensation from another Affiliate's sales.

No recruitment commissions, team overrides, or additional referral compensation shall apply unless separately authorized by LTC in a written agreement.

8. Intellectual Property & Promotional Content

All LTC trademarks, trade names, logos, product designs, marketing materials, photographs, and other proprietary assets remain the exclusive property of LTC or their respective owners.

LTC grants Affiliate a limited, nonexclusive, nontransferable, revocable license to use approved brand assets solely for authorized Program activities during the term of this Agreement.

Affiliate retains ownership of original promotional content created independently, subject to LTC's underlying intellectual property rights.

LTC may repost or share Affiliate's publicly posted promotional content using ordinary platform sharing features, subject to applicable platform rules.

Any broader use of Affiliate-created photographs, videos, testimonials, likenesses, or other original content in paid advertising or Company-owned promotional materials requires separate authorization or a signed Media Release.

Affiliate shall obtain all necessary permissions from individuals appearing in promotional content.

9. Confidentiality & Data Protection

Affiliate may receive access to nonpublic Company information, including marketing strategies, commission records, pricing information, internal processes, or business contacts.

Affiliate agrees not to disclose or misuse confidential Company information except as necessary to perform authorized Program activities.

Affiliate shall use customer and prospect information only for legitimate, authorized Program purposes and comply with applicable privacy and marketing laws.

Affiliate shall not export, sell, share, or independently exploit customer information obtained through LTC systems.

Upon termination, Affiliate shall discontinue use of Company systems and return or delete confidential information upon reasonable request, except where retention is legally required.

10. Independent Contractor Relationship

Affiliate participates as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, franchisee, joint venturer, or legal representative of LTC.

Affiliate controls the manner, timing, and methods of their promotional activities, subject to this Agreement and applicable law.

Affiliate is not entitled to employee benefits, wages, insurance, paid leave, or reimbursement of expenses unless expressly agreed in writing.

Affiliate is solely responsible for applicable taxes, reporting obligations, business licenses, and expenses associated with their activities.

Nothing in this Agreement guarantees minimum earnings, sales volume, or continued participation.

11. Term & Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon written or electronic notice to the other party.

LTC may immediately suspend or terminate Affiliate for fraud, misleading marketing, unauthorized marketplace activity, intellectual property misuse, material breach of this Agreement, or unlawful conduct.

Upon termination:

  • 1. Affiliate must immediately discontinue use of LTC affiliate links, trademarks, and promotional materials.
  • 2. Access to Company affiliate systems may be revoked.
  • 3. Valid commissions earned before the effective termination date remain payable in accordance with the normal payout cycle, subject to refunds, chargebacks, fraud review, and lawful offsets.
  • 4. No commissions accrue on sales occurring after termination unless LTC expressly agrees otherwise in writing.

Sections concerning confidentiality, intellectual property, unpaid commissions, liability, dispute resolution, and other provisions intended to survive shall remain effective.

12. Representations, Liability & Indemnification

Affiliate represents that they have the legal authority to enter into this Agreement and will comply with all applicable laws and Program requirements.

Affiliate agrees to indemnify and hold harmless LTC and its officers, employees, and representatives from third-party claims, losses, damages, and reasonable legal expenses arising from Affiliate's unlawful conduct, unauthorized representations, intellectual property infringement, or material breach of this Agreement, except to the extent caused by LTC's own misconduct.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages arising from participation in the Program.

Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

13. Program Modifications

LTC may update its Program procedures, approved marketing channels, tracking technology, brand guidelines, and commission policies upon reasonable notice.

Material changes to this Agreement shall be communicated electronically or in writing.

Continued participation after the stated effective date of a notified material change constitutes acceptance, where permitted by applicable law.

Changes to already-earned commissions shall not apply retroactively.

14. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

The parties agree to first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith discussions.

If a dispute cannot be resolved informally, either party may pursue available legal remedies in the state or federal courts having jurisdiction in the California county where LTC maintains its principal business office, subject to applicable law.

15. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the current LTC Brand Guidelines and any expressly incorporated written Program policies, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning Affiliate participation.

It supersedes prior discussions or representations regarding the same subject matter.

If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in effect.

No Affiliate may assign this Agreement without LTC's prior written consent.

Electronic signatures and electronically maintained copies shall have the same effect as original signatures to the extent permitted by law.
